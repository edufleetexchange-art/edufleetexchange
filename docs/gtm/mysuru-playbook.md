# Mysuru Launch Playbook — Teacher Placement Wedge

**Goal:** first paid teacher placement in Mysuru. Worksheet: `mysuru-target-list.csv`.

This is execution, not theory. Day = a conversation or a rupee, not a commit.

---

## Step 1 — Fill the worksheet (demand side)

Open `mysuru-target-list.csv`. The segment + area are pre-filled; you fill `name`
and `phone`. Run these searches and paste the real, current results:

**Google Maps** (most accurate phone numbers — tap the listing, copy the number):
- `tuition centre <area> Mysuru` (e.g. "tuition centre Kuvempunagar Mysuru")
- `coaching classes <area> Mysuru`
- `play school <area> Mysuru`, `preschool <area> Mysuru`, `daycare <area> Mysuru`
- `CBSE school Mysuru`, `ICSE school Mysuru`, `private school <area> Mysuru`

**JustDial:** search "tuition classes Mysore", "play schools Mysore", "schools Mysore"
— gives phone lists you can scan fast.

**Priority order (why):**
1. **Coaching/tuition centres** (rows 1–20) — hire year-round, owner picks up the
   phone, fast decisions. Your best shot at a quick first close.
2. **Preschools/daycares** (rows 21–35) — constant teacher/attendant turnover.
3. **K-12 private schools** (rows 36–50) — bigger fees but slower; main hiring
   wave is **Feb–June**. Seed relationships now, harvest then. Replacement/mid-year
   vacancies happen any time — ask anyway.

Target: **50 rows filled, 30 called, this week.**

---

## Step 2 — Seed the supply side (teachers) — free, do in parallel

You need teachers ready so you can say "I have candidates today." Sources in Mysuru:

- **WhatsApp/Telegram/Facebook groups** — search: "Mysore teachers jobs",
  "Mysuru teaching jobs", "Karnataka teacher jobs", "B.Ed Mysore". Join 8–10.
- **B.Ed / D.El.Ed colleges in Mysuru** — visit/call placement officers; ask to
  list graduating students. (Mysuru has several well-known teacher-training colleges
  — confirm current ones via "B.Ed college Mysuru" on Maps.)
- Post the teacher message (below). Goal: **50 teacher profiles** into the eduFleet
  roster (name, subject, experience, area, expected salary, phone).

When you add them to the platform, your **demand alerts** start firing — and so does
your founder "demand lead" inbox. That's the loop working.

---

## Step 3 — Scripts (Mysuru-tailored)

Offer the call in **Kannada or English** depending on who picks up — lead in Kannada
for coaching centres and preschools; English is fine for CBSE/ICSE school offices.

### Teacher recruitment (post in groups / WhatsApp)
> *"Namaskara 🙏 I place teachers in good schools and coaching centres across Mysuru —
> free for teachers. Looking for a teaching job? Send me: name, subject(s), years of
> experience, your area in Mysuru, and expected salary. I'll match you to schools that
> are hiring. DM me."*

### School / coaching-centre call (the only pitch that matters)
> *"Namaskara, I run a teacher placement service in Mysuru. I have screened, ready-to-join
> teachers — Maths, Science, English, primary, Kannada, whatever you need. Are you hiring
> any teachers right now, or expecting a vacancy soon? … I can send you 3 candidates today
> on WhatsApp — you interview them free, and you only pay me if you actually hire one.
> Shall I send them?"*

### After a hire (get the next customer)
> *"Glad it worked out! Whenever you have your next vacancy, just WhatsApp me one line and
> I'll send candidates the same day. And if you know another school or centre that's
> hiring, an introduction would mean a lot."*

---

## Step 4 — Offer & price (remove all risk)

- Free to post a vacancy, free to interview your candidates.
- **Pay only on hire:** flat fee per placement — start ~₹5,000–₹8,000 for a school
  teacher, less for preschool/coaching. Flat is easier to say yes to than "% of salary".
- **30-day free replacement** if the teacher leaves — this is what makes a stranger trust
  you. Teachers pay nothing, ever.

---

## Daily cadence (until first placement)

- **10 school/centre calls per day** (from the worksheet) → mark `vacancy` Y/N + `status`.
- **5 new teachers** added to the roster per day.
- When a vacancy appears: send 3 matched candidates same day → push to interview →
  push to offer → **invoice on hire.**

**The only metric that counts: a Mysuru school that paid you for one hire.** Get one,
then ten. Everything else is noise.
